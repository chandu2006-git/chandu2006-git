## About

I'm an Artificial Intelligence & Data Science student exploring how to turn machine-learning ideas into useful, testable systems. My work spans **GenAI and retrieval, machine learning, deep learning, and applied research**.

I care about more than a model's headline score: I want to understand how it behaves, where it fails, and what makes a system useful beyond a notebook.

- **GenAI:** domain-focused LLM experiments, multimodal retrieval, and AI workflows
- **ML & DL:** computer vision, classification, time-series learning, and evaluation
- **Engineering:** connecting models with data pipelines, interfaces, and reproducible project structure
- **Research mindset:** testing alternatives, documenting limitations, and learning from failure cases
